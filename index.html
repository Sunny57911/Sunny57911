Focus areas

Where climate science meets the energy grid

Four threads run through the work below: forecasting the atmosphere, correcting it against ground truth, modelling the energy systems it powers, and translating all of it into policy that governments can act on.

Weather & Climate Modelling

NWP / WRF-ARW simulations, cyclone-track modelling (MAHA, BULBUL), and CORDEX/IMD-based temperature & precipitation projections down to district and gram-panchayat level.

ML Bias-Correction

ANN, LSTM, XGBoost and stacked models trained to pull ERA5 reanalysis and downscaled solar/wind data closer to observed ground truth across South Asia.

Energy System Modelling

PyPSA-based demand projection and transition-pathway modelling for Jharkhand and Uttar Pradesh, including full state energy databases and renewable resource assessments.

Just Transition & Policy

Turning model output into evidence for state and national decarbonisation strategy — stakeholder roundtables, technical documentation, and Just Transition roadmaps.
