Industry research spanning energy-system modelling, dataset downscaling, and physical climate risk — each file below lists the datasets, methods and outcome.

01 Present · CEED

Energy System Modelling — Jharkhand & Uttar Pradesh

Full-stack state energy modelling: PyPSA-based system builds, block/district-level renewable resource assessment, and construction of the State Energy Database from the ground up. ERA5 and TerraClimate data were analysed to trace how climate patterns feed back into sector-level energy demand.

02 Past · Genesis Ray Energy

Downscaling & Bias-Corrected ERA5 — Solar/Wind, South Asia

A high-resolution downscaling technique for historical solar and wind data, refined with Kriging and NWP for fine-resolution accuracy. LSTM, XGBoost, ANN and stacked models were layered on top to bias-correct the downscaled ERA5 output for renewable-industry use.

03 Past · Genesis Ray Energy

Physical & Transition Risk Modelling

Continuous improvement of weather-data development across CMIP5, CMIP6 and CORDEX, paired with in-depth analysis of cyclonic events. Historical ERA5 data was used to characterise past storms, then combined with current weather patterns to project the likely paths of future tropical cyclones and depressions.
